# mBRSET

Source: https://physionet.org/content/mbrset/1.0/

## Documented contents
5,164 portable-camera fundus images from 1,291 patients, with demographic and clinical metadata.

## Access
Credentialed PhysioNet access, training, and DUA.

## Limits and pending audit
External evaluation only for compatible endpoints such as DR. Not an active-toxoplasmosis benchmark.
