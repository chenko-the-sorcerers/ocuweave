# BRSET v1.0.2

Source: https://physionet.org/content/brazilian-ophthalmological/1.0.2/

## Documented contents
16,266 color fundus images from 8,524 patients. Patient IDs, laterality, age, comorbidities text, insulin use, diabetes duration. Includes scar (toxoplasmosis) labels.

## Access
Credentialed PhysioNet access, required training, and DUA. Raw data cannot be redistributed.

## Limits and pending audit
Limited EMR-derived fields, not complete longitudinal EMR. Scar labels do not establish active infection.

Status: documentation reviewed; files not downloaded or audited.
