# Harvard-FairVLMed

Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/harvardairobotics/FairVLMed

## Documented contents
10,000 SLO images from 10,000 subjects with original notes and separate GPT-4 summaries. Glaucoma labels and demographics.

## Access
CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; non-commercial research. Check terms for derivative/model release.

## Limits and pending audit
Notes may name glaucoma or contain post-diagnostic treatment. Audit input timing and direct/indirect target leakage. SLO differs from color fundus.
