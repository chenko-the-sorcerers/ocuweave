\begin{abstract}
This working protocol describes the image-only segmentation stage of OcuWeave. We specify a compact query-based network, a residual local-detail extension, development-only data review, and controlled resolution and architecture comparisons. Nine randomly initialized runs are planned across three seeds. Multimodal learning with incomplete clinical records remains a separate, unimplemented research stage. This manuscript does not report completed LQNet experiments, independently validated clinical performance, or a peer-reviewed publication.
\end{abstract}
